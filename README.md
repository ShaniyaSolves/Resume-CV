# Resume & CV: Shaniya Jarrett

This repository contains my **resume and curriculum vitae** in the files above, along with selected work across research, industry, and outreach.

I'm a former engineer and current Astrophysics & Astronomy PhD researcher at the University of Maryland and NASA Goddard, with 7+ years of technical experience. I build deep learning models that find faint signals in noisy, real-world data, and I'm open to applying ML across domains, from tech, education, and healthcare to climate, media, and entertainment.

**Seeking:** internships in data science, machine learning, product management, or related roles.

---

## 🧠 Current Work: Machine Learning Research (NASA Goddard / UMD)

* Built a signal classification transformer from scratch on millions of time series to find high-priority targets for a $1.5B NASA mission, cutting false positives **3x** compared to standard methods.
* Diagnosed and parallelized a bottleneck in the training data pipeline, speeding up training **6x** and benchmarking memory scaling to size supercomputer runs.
* Validated results with frequentist and Bayesian statistics (hypothesis tests, likelihood-based model comparison) and ran ablation, saliency, and spurious-correlation checks to rule out artifacts.
* Fine-tuned a pre-trained OpenAI audio/speech recognition transformer on GPUs for low signal-to-noise data.

**Tools:** Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face, Scikit-learn, SciPy, Pandas, SQL, Git, AWS

## 📊 Earlier Research

* **Master's research (Fisk / Vanderbilt):** took a project from concept to publication, applying correlation and clustering analysis to 1 TB of simulation data to find a low-cost proxy for predicting trends across a much larger dataset.
* **Undergraduate research (LASP / CU Boulder):** developed anomaly detection and trend analysis methods for 4 NASA missions, built a predictive model from a newly discovered nonlinear relationship between two signals, and ran ray-tracing simulations that sharpened satellite images 20%.
